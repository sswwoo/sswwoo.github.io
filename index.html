About

I'm a Ph.D. student at KAIST Multimodal AI Lab, advised by Professor Joon Son Chung. My research focuses on multimodal AI, with a particular interest in grounding physical properties in visual scenes, enabling AI systems to understand not only what objects look like, but also how they hear, feel, smell, and interact with the real world.
